FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

Quick answers about managing stores, themes, integrations, and domains.

How fast can I launch my online store? +
You can launch in under 2 minutes. Sign up, pick your preferred storefront theme, upload your first product with drag & drop media, and your store is immediately live and ready to take orders.
Can I customize the storefront design and layout? +
Yes. OmniStore provides customizable themes (Nova, Luxe, Mincom, Funo), visual mega menus, banner customizers, and customizable color palettes — all editable directly inside the CMS without touching code.
Which communication channels are supported for order updates? +
The Apps Marketplace supports Wati WhatsApp, Meta WhatsApp Cloud API, Mailgun, custom SMTP mailboxes (Google Workspace, Zoho, Outlook), and Twilio SMS. All order confirmations, tracking numbers, and refund receipts are dispatched automatically.
Can I connect multiple stores under a single account? +
Yes! OmniStore is built on a multi-tenant engine. You can create, switch between, and govern multiple brand stores, each with its own isolated catalog, currency, orders, and custom domain, all from a single login.
